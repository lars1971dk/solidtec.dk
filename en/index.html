Services

Two core services with one shared goal: a more efficient production

Our expertise is PLC and SCADA software development. We use it for two things: automating your processes and turning your production data into a decision-making tool.

Process automation

We create efficient workflows and improve your productivity through process automation. The solutions are scalable and flexible, adapted to your needs and built to grow with your business.

  • PLC software development for controlling production processes
  • Tailored to your plant, not an off-the-shelf product
  • Scalable: the solution can be extended as your needs change

Data collection & analysis

We offer advanced data collection and analysis that strengthens your decision-making. With SCADA software you get an overview of production and a fact-based foundation for optimisation.

  • SCADA software for monitoring production
  • Production data in one place instead of scattered across systems
  • A basis for finding bottlenecks and optimising processes

About SOLIDTEC

Automation Services from Herning, Denmark, since 2003

SOLIDTEC was founded in Herning, Denmark, with the vision of delivering tailored automation solutions. Our expertise lies in developing PLC and SCADA software that optimises production processes across industries.

Through dedication and continuous development, the company has grown into an experienced supplier. The team in Herning works purposefully to deliver high-quality automation solutions that meet customer needs and ensure efficiency and reliability in every process.
